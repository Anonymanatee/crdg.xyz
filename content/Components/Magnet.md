## Overview

The magnet is a magnetic object that attracts metal objects. It is Blue and Red with a white S and N on the South and North sides respectively. It is spawned from the Component Machine.

## Usage

The magnet allows you to attract any metal items towards it, if you burn off 1 half of it then it becomes way more powerful, as well as if you craft it with another magnet.
